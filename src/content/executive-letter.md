# Letter from the Executive Director

![Jennifer Kelley headshot](/Staff/jennifer-kelley_orig.jpg)

We are celebrating our **25th year** as an organization! We have come a long way in a short time in building a quality organization that helps the citizens of Glascock County.

The information outlined in this annual report reflects a commitment on the part of the **75+ collaborative members** and our **7 executive board members** of Family Connection and Communities In Schools of Glascock County, Inc. As a community collaborative, we succeed in our mission to create a quality of life for all citizens of Glascock County that supports and encourages the development of healthy, stable families.

We continue to be successful in securing grant funds from a variety of sources to provide numerous programs in the community. In **FY 2024 we brought in $710,350** in grant funds. We have increased our funding partners from **one** partner in our first year to **thirteen** partners in our 25th year. We will continue to seek additional funding on an ongoing basis with the hope of maintaining and expanding our current program services and activities.

I feel very blessed to live and work in such a wonderful community as Glascock County. One could search the world over and not find a greater group of people than right here in Glascock County. I also know I am truly blessed—as each of you are—to have the greatest group of staff at Family Connection and Communities In Schools of Glascock County, Inc. I have the utmost pride in all of our staff and truly appreciate the team approach that each of them carries each day. The staff truly makes my job so much fun; I look forward to coming to work each and every day.

I look forward to another year of success and commend all the members of the collaborative who have dedicated their time and effort to making Family Connection and Communities In Schools of Glascock County, Inc. a model collaborative.

**Jennifer Kelley**  
Executive Director  
Family Connection and Communities In Schools of Glascock County, Inc.
