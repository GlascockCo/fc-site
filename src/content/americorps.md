# AmeriCorps Volunteers (K–3)

**Made possible by Communities In Schools of Georgia.**  
AmeriCorps members serve Glascock County for a **10-month term** to strengthen support for our youngest learners and local families.

## What volunteers do
- Provide **remedial assistance and tutoring** for students in **K–3** during the after-school program
- Help increase independent reading time and strengthen literacy and numeracy skills
- Assist families with **Energy Assistance applications**
- Support the **Clothes Closet** and **Food Pantry** as needed

## Impact
This work helps raise reading and math scores, strengthens academic skills, and supports higher graduation rates for Glascock County students.

## Time commitment & benefits
- **Approx. 25 hours/week** during the 10-month term  
- **Living-allowance stipend** paid **every two weeks**  
- **Education award** available at the end of service (for college expenses)

## Training & support
- Orientation and ongoing coaching
- Materials and lesson guides are provided

## Eligibility
- 18+ years old
- Background check required
- Reliable, positive, and patient with young learners
